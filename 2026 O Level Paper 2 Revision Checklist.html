Before you hand in Paper 2

  1. Show your working. One step per line, so the marker can follow it.
  2. Answers to 3 significant figures, angles to 1 decimal place, unless the question says otherwise.
  3. Use your calculator's π or 3.142.
  4. Keep full values in your calculator. Only round at the final answer.
  5. Check DEG or RAD mode before every trig question.
  6. The marks in [ ] tell you roughly how many steps are needed.
  7. Write units on every answer (cm², km/h, $, °).
  8. Graph question: smooth curve, no ruler joins, label your line.
  9. Real-world question: end with a clear decision and the numbers that support it.
